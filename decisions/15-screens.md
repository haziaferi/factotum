# 15. Screens, navigation and wording (§10.1)

Status: **T0 answers recorded 2026-10-05.** The working brief is `docs/screens-workflow-prompt.md`; it was measured at 38/38.

## How screens are settled

The owner decided this on 2026-10-05.

- **Tier by tier, in dependency order:**
  - T0 frame;
  - T1 shared parts;
  - T2 items, calendar and alarms;
  - T3 habits, trackers, time, goals, charts and checklists;
  - T4 check-in and regulation;
  - T5 pages, databases, canvas, journal and templates;
  - T6 search, settings, sync, lock, backup, onboarding and about.
- **Questions:** one batch per tier, each with its evidence and what it blocks.
- **Mockups:**
  - An in-repo HTML page per screen, with a phone frame and a desktop frame.
  - Each sits beside screenshots of the source apps, taken from debug builds on empty or seed data and kept out of git in `_screens-sources/`.
  - Each lists what it leaves out.
- **Building:** a tier is built only after its mockups are agreed.
- **Imports:** port Tendril's commonMain UI. Rewrite Chronicle, Mnemo and Equipoise UI from its behaviour, since it is Android-only. Every import passes code-verification-core, /code-review, /simplify and an AI-slop sweep.

  - this PC's `~/.android/debug.keystore` starts `74:BF:E2:95…`, and Mnemo and Equipoise use it.

**Measured** (compare.html), for the palette scoring to beat:
- Mnemo's default primary on its background is 2.0:1 in light and 2.9:1 in dark, under the 3:1 a control needs.
- Tendril's outline is about 1.2:1 in light and 1.4:1 in dark. Tendril uses it as a hairline divider.

**Use by purpose** (`docs/screens/t0/jobs.md`, owner 2026-10-05). The design fixes these cadences:
- **Daily:** the journal, agenda and calendar, habits, checklists, the ledger and readings.
- **Several times a day:** tasks, "n a day" habits and search.
- **Weekly:** the planner.

The owner set the rest:
- pages, databases and canvas are **daily on both devices**;
- looking back is a **weekly review**;
- a check-in happens **when something is noticed**.

Prompts that arrive (alarms, clashes, outcome questions) cost navigation nothing.

**Features (owner, 2026-10-05).** These move across: Android widgets, the on-device LLM, a Windows global quick-add, and natural-language input where appropriate. **Tendril's opt-in Claude text verbs are dropped**, an owner-accepted exception to §0.1.5.
