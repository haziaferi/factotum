T2: items, calendar and alarms

The approved screens (decision 18, owner, 2026-10-12 and 13), in greys, placeholder data in dotted outlines. The picks and their controls are in t2-options.html and t2-scores.html; every option's scores are in the parts' JSON records, the reasons in decisions/18-screens-t2.md.

Calendar: Week (the default)

Tabs Week · Month · 3 days. Week is the planner: the days as cells, each listing its things with their start times; a tapped day opens the Day screen.

Calendar: Month

A tapped date opens the Day screen.

Calendar: 3 days

The time grid, titles wrapping in their blocks, clashes side by side, the line at the current time.

The Day screen

Opened from a day in Week or Month: all the hours down the left, opened at the day's first thing, the line at the current time, every thing a block with its times, the untimed in a strip above; chips show one kind.

An item's page

The row grows in place, every field shown (an unset one reads "Add a reminder"), each change kept as made with Undo.

The Reminders list

Soonest first under day headers. A tap grows the row in place, as an item's page does; Skip for today sits under When, and the skipped row folds into "Done today".

The alarm ringing

Full screen over the lock screen and any app: the time, what it is, one large Snooze, and Slide to dismiss at the foot. Skip for today comes an hour before, in a quiet notification.
