Module palettes: 12 groups

Each row is one group: Calendar, Routines and check-in, each with primary, secondary and tertiary (large chips), their containers (narrow chips) and the five-step chart scale. Open a row to see the palettes on a Calendar week, a Routines screen with a chart and a heatmap, and check-in with the ledger and the trend. Chart lines for trackers use colours from the guided picker (series.md), labelled directly, not the module palette. 145 groups passed every check; these are chosen for harmony, separation and variety. Pages is not part of this.
