Factotum navigation: every candidate

These are structure mockups for decision 15, Q2. They show where things live and how many steps they cost, not how they look. Greys, the system font and the glyphs are placeholders, because the palette, type, icons and wording are still to be scored. Every candidate uses the same invented sample data. The numbers come from tools/screens/nav_score2.py, "front" marks the Pareto front, and the candidates are ordered by total weekly cost.

What these mockups leave out

  • Look: the palette, type, shapes, density and icons (Q4–Q8). Every grey, size and glyph is a placeholder.
  • Wording: registers are per cluster and set in their tiers (Q9). The words here are placeholders, except "Label", "Log" and "check-in".
  • Prompts that arrive: alarms, clashes, the outcome question, the timer question, burnout.
  • Other screens: the editors, the [+] sheet (shown in frame.html), search results, widgets, the tray, empty states, and anything behind a second tap other than what is drawn.
  • The on-device LLM is not placed in any candidate.
  • The source screenshots are on frame.html, so they are not repeated here.
