Home screen workshop

Structure only. Colours, type and icons are placeholders until Q4–Q8 are scored. The sample data is invented. Each element shows how often you use it per week (35 = several a day, 7 = daily, 2 = when noticed).

1. Agenda, Today and Day

Today is the home screen, the whole day across modules. Agenda is one part of Today: what is due, as a list in time order. Day is a calendar view: an hour grid, events drawn as long as they last, for gaps and clashes. It sits with Week and Month, not on home.

2. Build the home screen

Tick what belongs, reorder with the arrows, pick a day layout and where check-in sits. The phone updates as you go. When it looks right, copy the summary at the bottom and paste it back to me.
