Module palettes: 24 groups

Calendar, Routines and check-in, one palette each; Pages keeps its amber. Every colour is computed by tools/screens/palette_groups.py from the lexicon picks in palette-keywords.json, and checked on all three grounds. 111 groups passed the separation and colour-vision checks; these are the most harmonious, most distinct ones, chosen to differ from each other. Each module row shows its accent (filled) and its tint. The mini home applies the group.
