# Navigation at a glance

Generated by `tools/screens/nav_glance.py` (its docstring states the model).

144 variants: every combination of bar (modules, pins, min), ⊞ form (flat, cards, folders, grid), top row (full, top3, hide, none) and coding (plain, hue, depth). Each was drawn as three phone screens, start, a module and ⊞, then rendered headless at 360×740. **Clutter** is measured: the share of edge pixels, averaged over the three. **Find in ⊞** is a search model: the items you inspect to find a feature. **Full contrast** counts the navigation elements drawn at full strength on the start screen. Step costs come from `nav_score2`.
