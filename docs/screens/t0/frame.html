  • Greys, the system font and the placeholder glyphs (◷ ▦ ☰ …) stand in for the palette, type and icons, which are not decided (Q4–Q8).
  • Words are placeholders, except "Label", "Log" and "check-in".
  • All content is invented sample data.
  • Step costs are the ones in nav-scores.md, and the mockup is built to match them.

What this mockup leaves out

  • Look: the palette, type, shapes, density and icons (Q4–Q8). Every grey, size and glyph here is a placeholder.
  • Wording: registers are per cluster and set in their tiers (Q9). The words here are placeholders.
  • Prompts that arrive: alarms, missed alarms, sync clashes, "how did that tool go?", the 12-hour timer question and the burnout notice. None costs navigation.
  • Other screens:
    • settings (two sections, behind the account button);
    • trash, lock, backup, onboarding and about;
    • the editors behind each row;
    • search results;
    • the check-in, ledger and sensory flows behind their chips;
    • Android widgets;
    • the Windows tray;
    • empty states;
    • dark mode, where the page follows the system in greys only.
  • Open questions:
    • whether "Logged on N of 7 days" in Review breaks §0.1.3 (a T3 question);
    • where the on-device LLM lives (it is not placed yet).
