Rules

  • If · After · Before · When · During, then the premise; "then" and the habit; the days; the time; the energy (owner).
  • Every day is the default and is written as "every day" here, but never on the list (owner).
  • "Available ifs" (owner): a small line above the sentence. It opens upward and fills the if, its days and its time. The habit then joins that if: an if with two or more habits is a routine.
  • A joined if's days and time are the if's: they read dotted, and changing them changes the if for every habit on it.
  • The time: no set time, at a time, or from a time to a time (on the dial with a typed field). A window may run past midnight.
  • Add is drawn only once the if's premise and the habit are written.
