T1.7 Labels approved

A label is a coloured dot, not a chip: it takes no room from titles and adds no height. The name is read in the filter bar, on the item's page, in the picker and in Settings › Labels, whose list doubles as the colour legend. Every variant, its measures and a control per part: labels-scores.html.
