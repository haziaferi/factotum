Rules (owner, 2026-10-08)

  • Minutes and hours take only a window ("between a and b") or all day: the interval sets the times.
  • At random (random gaps) needs a time bound: a fixed time, or a window the moment falls in ("every 2–4 days at random between 9:00 and 12:00"); "on [days]" is hidden.
  • A random time takes a count per day: "at 3 random times between 9:00 and 17:00".
  • After it's done hides "on [days]"; its time is optional.
  • Ends: forever, until, n times in all; minutes and hours also "for n days". A finite end reads back its last occurrence.
  • Impossible options are not drawn; until cannot be before the start.
