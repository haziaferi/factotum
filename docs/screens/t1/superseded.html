T1.5 Date and time picker (superseded by the When sheet) approved: Dial + typed

Typing sets the date and time; a typed date is never asked again, and tapping its highlight offers only Keep as text. The picker is opened by the person, from ⋯ › Date beside the line or the full page's Date row, straight on the month (owner, 2026-10-08). Two steps in a sheet: the month with its day load, then the time and the event's end. Week start and the 12/24 h clock follow the system. Four tasks: Fri 16; Fri 16 at 14:30; an event Fri 16 at 15:00 for 1 h; the 3rd of next month. Scores: tools/screens/picker3_opt.py (60 variants), picker_desk.py (Windows), docs/screens/t1/picker3-opt.json, picker3-actions.json. Repeat (cron and its calculator) is T1.6.
