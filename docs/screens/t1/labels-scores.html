Every variant scored, generated from the measured files (labels-a…f.json) by python tools/screens/label_opt.py scores. Measures come from headless Edge at 360×740: targets, reach, row heights, and clutter rendered light and dark. Measures

  • title_room: px the title keeps (more is better)
  • extra_h: px added to a row
  • rows_lost: rows pushed off Today
  • hidden: labels not shown
  • lines: extra lines
  • truncated: names cut
  • min_text: smallest text px
  • apart: label kinds told apart (of 3; more is better)
  • clutter: edge density, light and dark averaged (lower is calmer)
  • ref_distance: share of the 8 captured apps that draw it differently
  • actions: taps and keys over the part's tasks
  • reach: Fitts ID from the thumb (lower is easier)
  • covered: share of the screen covered
  • controls: controls on screen
  • labels_seen: labels visible, of 12
  • line_move: px the capture line moves when suggestions appear
  • shown: suggestions visible
  • line_hidden: 1 = the capture line is covered while choosing
  • controls_per_row: controls on each Settings row
  • see_all: actions to see everything carrying a label, and back
  • complete: 1 = the view shows every kind (tasks, pages, entries)
  • exposure: tap area per row, share of the row
  • chips: chips that are targets
  • column: px the dots stray from one vertical line
