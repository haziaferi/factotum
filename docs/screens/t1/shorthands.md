| Token | Kind | Example | Clashes with a word | Status |

| `ev` | event | `ev dentist tomorrow 9am` | no | kept |

## Field markers (typed anywhere)

| Marker | Field | Example | Means elsewhere |
|---|---|---|---|
| `#` | label | `#health` | TickTick: tag; Todoist: project |
| `!` | reminder offset | `!10m` | Todoist: reminder; TickTick: priority |
| `@` | calendar, or the tracker a log goes to | `@personal · @water` | Todoist: label (being retired); TickTick: assignee |
| `^` | page the item links to | `^garden plan` | TickTick: list |
| `~` | duration | `~30m` | nothing in the references |
| `*` | repeat | `*every 6 months` | TickTick: date |

## How many fit in the hint

- One line at 12 px holds **5 of the 8 kinds** (j journal · lg log · t timer · ck check-in · td task).
- The 6 field markers fit **5 per line**.
- All 14 need **3 lines** of hint.
