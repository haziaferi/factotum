Paced timer: try the sheets (T3b.4e)

Breaks are a clause of the sentence: with [N] [chimes | rests of [X min]] evenly. Rests spread evenly take their length out of the run, so the stretches of work between them are equal. One break can be set apart, at a time or at a fraction (⅓). The ring shows the run and its breaks; tap a break to select it, then move it on the dial, with − / +, or by a slowed drag, depending on the sheet. On the "rough" sheets a press on an empty part of the ring places a chime, snapping to ½, ⅓, ¼, ⅙, ⅛ near them. Moving one break of an evenly spread group fixes the whole group where it is. Time boxes open the dial at once (⌨ types instead); counts are typed with the phone's keyboard. Tasks follow the time you press Begin.
