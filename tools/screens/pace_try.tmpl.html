Paced timer: try the sheets (T3b.4d)

On the dial sheets, a time box opens a real dial at once. A clock time is set hours first (the 24-hour dial: 1–12 outside, 13–00 inside), then minutes; a length on one face, hours on the outer ring and minutes on the inner one, adding up. The boxes stay writable: ⌨ on the dial types instead, with the phone's keyboard. On the typed sheet the boxes are plain fields for the phone's keyboard. The ring is always the whole run, at any length: its places are fractions of the run. Tasks follow the time you press Begin. Taps, keystrokes and drags are counted apart; Start checks the run (a pause, or a rest's length, may be half a minute off).
