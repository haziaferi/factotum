The running Paced timer: try where it lives (T3b.4g)

Two timers run at once: Reading, a Paced run with 2 rests of 5 min evenly, and Cleaning, a plain timer. The clock runs at real speed. Every task starts on Today; tap a timer's card (›) to open its running view. Press Begin, do the task in the phone, then press Done: the page checks it, and a Done too early counts as an error. Taps in the phone are counted. Each running card has ‖ / ▶ (pause, resume) and ■ (stop). On "hold" sheets, Stop needs a press held for half a second; any stop can be undone for 10 seconds, in the card itself.
